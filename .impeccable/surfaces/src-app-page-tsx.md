# Home (/)

Scope: the landing/home route. Mode: Persuade (adapts to returning players).
Audience: Commander players arriving from a friend's link (new), and the owner's playgroup returning with a collection already imported.
Action: new visitor drops/pastes their collection into the working import on the first screen; returning player brews a deck (Build / Roll) straight away.
Proof: real mechanism only — cards in, deck out, reasons shown. No invented user counts, testimonials or ratings.
Constraints: navy/orange pixel-fantasy world, readability requirement (body text readable; Aa toggle), reduced motion respected, no boats.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is the cauldron: your bulk goes in, a finished deck comes out. It refuses the category default of a centred headline over a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: A night castle courtyard in the navy ramp, lit by one hot orange source (the brew and its fire) that rim-lights every object, with cream moonlight from the upper left. Objects are pixel-art with four-tone shading (shadow, base, light, highlight), drawn on one art grid and scaled with nearest-neighbour. Pixelify type, the Press Start wordmark, stepped borders.

STORY: The visitor watches binder cards tumble into the brew and a sleeved deck rise out. They believe it only ever uses what they own and explains why. Then they drop their collection into the cauldron, or a returning player brews a deck now.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The cauldron scene fills the right ~60% on flagstones, with fire beneath, cards spiralling in from a binder at the left edge, and a deck box rising from the brew. The left column holds the headline, one line of subcopy, and the working import (drop or paste) as the primary action. A returning visitor sees monumental pixel counters (cards owned, decks brewed) with Build and Roll instead. A numbered 1·2·3 path (Bulk → Brew → Deck) sits under the fold line. On mobile the scene is cropped to the cauldron, with the action still inside the first screen.
Signature interaction: dropping a file makes the brew flare and the import starts. Cards keep circling while it runs.
Motion grammar: stepped frame tables at 10 fps, no easing.
Raise (anime command wall): monumental pixel counters. Raise (origami sequence): the strictly numbered transformation path.

FORM: Brewing cauldron, #1 on my ordered list. The user chose it over the dealt hand (realm map, live demo, grimoire). seed:fcaefa2a8ae3bd56
